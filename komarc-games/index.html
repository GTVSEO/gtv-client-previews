Built to Be Played

Komarc products are designed to come back out at the next party, family dinner, classroom, or game night.

  • Cool Products. Real Play.

    Play with family, friends, classmates, or colleagues at home, at parties, in schools, or at the office.

  • Hands-On Fun

    A little less screen time… a lot more face time. Balance blocks, fling sticky snacks, grab rings, or solve patterns.

  • Years of Development Behind Every Product

    It can take 6 months to 3 years to bring a Komarc product to market, from concept and prototyping to testing, tooling, and production.

How Komarc Products Get Made

Komarc operates manufacturing facilities and long-term production partnerships across Vietnam and China, with headquarters in Hong Kong and North American operations in Logan, Utah.

All Komarc products meet major international safety and quality standards:

  • ASTM
  • CPSIA
  • EN71
  • REACH
  • CE
  • FSC

  • Wood Manufacturing

    Games, toys, and gifts in Acacia, Rubberwood, Beech, White Oak, Brazilian Pine, and other hardwoods.

  • Plastic Manufacturing

    Injection molding in ABS, Acrylic, PP, PU, PVC, PS, and other materials.

  • Printing & Packaging

    Rigid boxes, folding cartons, game boards, card decks, inserts, and retail displays.
